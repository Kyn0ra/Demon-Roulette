**Demon Roulette** is a Geometry Dash Geode mod that randomly picks demons for you to play!

Can't decide what demon to beat next? Let Demon Roulette choose your next challenge. Spin the roulette, get a random demon and see if you can beat it!

Made for players looking for new challenges, discovering demons and escaping the endless cycle of choosing what to play.

Good luck, and may the roulette be in your favour!
